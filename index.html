How to play

Clear the board by removing tiles in matching pairs.

  • You can only take a free tile: nothing may lie on top of it, and its left or right side must be open.
  • Identical tiles match. Any Flower matches any other Flower, and any Season matches any other Season.
  • You win when the board is empty. If no free pair is left, you are stuck. Undo, or shuffle the remaining tiles.
  • Every new deal can be won.

The 144 tiles

  • Dots, Bamboo, Characters: numbered 1–9, four of each.
  • Winds (East, South, West, North) and Dragons (Red, Green, White): four of each.
  • Flowers and Seasons: one of each, four of each kind.
